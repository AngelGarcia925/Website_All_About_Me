My Unhealthy Obsession

Why is this considered an "Unhealthy" Obsession?

I'd consider this game to have been an "Unhealthy" Obsession in the past, but not necessarily at this moment. Currently, I've played the game far less, however, when I was younger, it was certainly a bit of an obsession. It was during that stint that I was probably the most into the game, putting in very well around a thousand hours or so.

How long have I been playing this game on/off?

I've been playing the game since I was roughly seven or so, starting on the Pocket Edition, before moving onto console when I was 9 and got my first console in the form of a Nintendo Switch. Since then however I've mainly played on PC for the last several years since I got one.

What got me into the game and kept me playing?

Initially, the reason I played the game so much was simply because it was one of the only games I knew how to. This was because when I first started, I played on the phone edition, which was much easier for me to pick up than on console or computer, since I hadn't really used either medium before. Since then however, what has kept me playing has been building, which is where most of my time has been spent, pictured on the right are two builds I made earlier this year.

What have I done to keep it from getting boring?

While I have tried numerous methods of keeping the game fresh, the best way I've managed to still enjoy the game is through modpacks, which for me add a lot to the game. While vanilla survival can get boring after long enough due to feeling like I've done everything, in a modded world, there's usually more to do.
